Frameworks Studios is a premier immersive digital marketing agency specialising in luxury bespoke 360° virtual reality tours and interactive immersive digital brochures. In today's digital-first world, immersive experiences are no longer a luxury — they are essential.

We craft visually stunning, fully immersive tours that elevate brand presence, deepen audience engagement and directly accelerate enquiries, bookings and sales. Every experience integrates seamlessly into your existing campaigns, adding depth and interactivity that resonate with today's experience-driven audiences — all fully aligned with your unique brand identity.

WHAT WE DO

Everything around the space, under one roof.

The 360° tour is the centrepiece. These are the three disciplines that surround it — shot by the same crews, finished to the same brief, and delivered together rather than chased across three suppliers.

Architectural and interior sets, property and lifestyle photography, product and detail work, and aerial stills where the site calls for it. Styling and digital staging where a room needs dressing, then retouching to your brand guidelines rather than to a house look. Delivered as a licensed library, cropped for the channels it is going to live on — not one master you have to cut down yourself.
