THE STUDIO

BORN IN MONACO. BUILT AROUND EXPERIENCE.

01THE STUDIO

Founded in Monaco in 2018 on a simple belief:

EXTRAORDINARY PLACES DESERVE EXTRAORDINARY EXPERIENCES.

We set out to transport people there long before arrival.

THAT BELIEF BECAME FRAMEWORKS.

02THE PHILOSOPHY

WE ARE NOT DEFINED BY THE TECHNOLOGY WE USE, BUT BY THE EXPERIENCES WE CREATE.

Every project begins with the real world — the architecture, the light, the detail that make a place distinctive. Technology preserves those qualities; it should never become the experience.

03THE STUDIO TODAY

DIFFERENT DISCIPLINES. ONE CREATIVE VISION.

A global collective across real-world capture, immersive media, storytelling, audio, design, AI and technology.

Each brings a perspective. Together they see the whole experience — first image to final interaction.

04BESPOKE BY NATURE

NOTHING TEMPLATED. NOTHING IMPOSED.

Every commission is shaped by the identity, ambition and character of the brand behind it.

We listen, interpret and create from the inside out — a visual language and rhythm that could belong to no one else.

05THE DIRECTION

THE WAY PEOPLE EXPERIENCE PLACES IS CHANGING.

The first encounter with a destination no longer begins at the entrance. It begins on a screen, long before a journey is made.

Frameworks exists to lead that evolution — creating more meaningful ways for people to connect with the world's most remarkable places.
